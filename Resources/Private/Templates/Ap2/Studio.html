What is AP2?

AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol) answers the hardest question in agentic commerce: how does a merchant know an agent is really allowed to pay? The answer is a chain of signed mandates — verifiable credentials that carry the human's authorization to the agent, the merchant and the payment network.

An Intent Mandate sets the limits while the human is present; a Cart Mandate locks a specific, fully-priced order; verification proves the cart is within the intent and untampered. Here the tokens are sandbox-signed with a demo key — genuine JWS mechanics, no real identity or money.
