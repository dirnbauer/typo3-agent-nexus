What “verify the chain” checks

  • Intent Mandate signature — the token is intact and signed (HS256, sandbox key).
  • Cart Mandate signature — likewise; tampering breaks it.
  • Cart references the Intent — the cart's intentRef equals the intent's jti.
  • Same authorized merchant — the cart's audience is one the intent allows.
  • Within the spending cap — the cart total ≤ the intent's maxAmountCents.
  • Not expired — both exp timestamps are in the future.

Only when every check passes is a (simulated) payment authorized.
