The issuer key of the open mandates ("agent-provider-key-1") and the merchant key
of the checkout JWT ("merchant-key-1") are not published, so those two signatures
cannot be checked. Everything else can: the closed mandates' key-binding
signatures under the open mandates' cnf.jwk, sd_hash, checkout_hash,
transaction_id and the payment reference.
