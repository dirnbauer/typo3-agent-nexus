1.0/a2a.json
    The non-normative JSON Schema (draft 2020-12) generated from that proto, as published at
    https://a2a-protocol.org/dev/spec/a2a.json (built from main a9c7f4c20e990d13d3c3bf33b0fbf0443448c60c).
    The v1.0.1 bundle at https://a2a-protocol.org/v1.0.1/spec/a2a.json has unresolvable $refs
    (upstream issue #2073, fixed on main by #2074); this build is otherwise identical in content.
    The generated schema carries no `required` lists - required fields are taken from the proto
    and asserted separately by the tests.
