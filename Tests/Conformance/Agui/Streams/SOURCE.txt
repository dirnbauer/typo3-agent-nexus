The files are unchanged. Each one is a stream a *client* must either consume or reject.
OfficialStreamsTest replays them through Agui\Protocol\EventVerifier, which checks *producer*
streams: every stream a client must reject, the verifier rejects too; every stream a client
consumes as it is, the verifier accepts. Streams a client only survives by stripping
unrecognised material are rejected, because a producer must never send such material.
